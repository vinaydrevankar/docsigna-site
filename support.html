Frequently asked questions

How do I sign a PDF?

Tap Open PDF on the home screen and choose a file. Tap Add signature, pick a saved signature, then drag, pinch and twist it into place. Tap Save to create the signed copy.

How do I add my signature?

Sign on plain white paper with dark ink, then tap New (or Add new signature) and take a photo or choose one from your files. DocSigna removes the paper background so only the ink remains.

My signature wasn't detected, or part of the paper is still showing.

Use plain white paper and good, even light. Avoid shadows across the page, and keep the signature large in the frame. Try again with a darker pen.

Can I use more than one signature?

Yes. Save as many as you like, rename them in My signatures, and choose one each time you sign.

Where are my signed documents?

They appear under Recent documents on the home screen. Open one to view it, or use the menu to share or delete it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete signatures in My signatures and signed documents from the document menu. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. DocSigna has no account and does not keep your files on a server.

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. Signing happens on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
