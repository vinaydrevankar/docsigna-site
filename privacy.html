DocSigna Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The short version

DocSigna signs PDF documents on your phone. There are no accounts. Your documents and signatures are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

  • Your PDF documents. The app reads the PDFs you choose and saves signed copies in the app's storage on your device.
  • Your signatures. Photos of your signature are processed on your device to remove the background. The result is saved locally so you can reuse it.
  • Your settings and recent documents list. These are saved locally.

Removing the app deletes this data. You can also delete individual signatures and signed documents inside the app.

Camera and photos

If you choose to photograph your signature or pick an image of it, DocSigna asks for camera or photo access. Images are used only to extract your signature and are processed on your device. The app does not upload them.

Information collected automatically

We use Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. When the app crashes, it may send technical information such as the device model, operating system version, app version, the state of the app at the time of the crash, and an anonymous installation identifier. It does not include the contents of your documents or signatures. We use this only to diagnose and fix problems.

We use Expo Updates to deliver app updates. When the app checks for an update, a request is made to Expo's servers that includes technical details such as the app version and platform, and your IP address is visible to the server as with any internet request.

Google's handling of Crashlytics data is described in the Firebase privacy information. Expo's is described in the Expo privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. When you share a signed PDF using your phone's share sheet, it goes to the app or person you choose. That sharing is controlled by you.

Children

DocSigna is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete your signatures and signed documents in the app, or delete the app to remove all local data.
  • Turn off camera or photo access at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • To ask about crash data tied to your installation, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
